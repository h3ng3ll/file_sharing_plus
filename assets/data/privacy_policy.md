# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-28_

File Sharing is a local Wi-Fi file-transfer app. Your Mac runs a server that shares a folder you
choose, and your iPhone connects to it over the same network to browse, download, and upload files.

## No data leaves your device

The app works **entirely on your local Wi-Fi network**. There is no cloud, no online account, no
remote server, and no internet backend of any kind. Your files and folders never pass through us or
any third party — they move **directly** between your Mac and your iPhone on your own network.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Specifically, the app
contains:

- No analytics or usage tracking.
- No telemetry or crash reporting.
- No advertising or third-party SDKs.
- No device identifiers, contacts, or location data.

Nothing about you, your device, or your files is ever sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What is stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data **locally on your device only**, so it can remember your setup
between launches:

- The path of the folder you chose to share (on the Mac).
- The network port the server uses.
- A history of your transfers (file names, direction, and timestamps).

This data stays on your device, is never uploaded, and is removed if you delete the app. You can clear
your transfer history from within the app at any time.

## Permissions

- **Local network access** (Bonjour / mDNS) — used only to discover Macs running the app on your
  Wi-Fi network. It is not used for any other purpose.
- **File access** — used only when you pick a folder to share or a file to send, and to save files
  you download.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new release of the app and the date above
will be updated.
