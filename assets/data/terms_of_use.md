## What the app does

File Sharing lets your Mac share a folder you choose over your local Wi-Fi network. While the server is
running, devices on the same network can browse, download, upload, and delete files in that folder
through the app.

## Open access on your network

The app is designed for convenience on a network you trust. You should understand that:

- **There is no password and no encryption.** Connections use plain HTTP and WebSocket traffic.
- While the server is running, **any device on the same Wi-Fi network can browse, download, upload,
  and permanently delete** files in the shared folder — not only your own iPhone.
- Traffic on the network is unencrypted and could be observed by other devices on that network.

**Use the app only on private, trusted networks** — for example your home Wi-Fi — and turn the server
off when you are finished.

## Deletions are permanent

Files deleted through the app are removed **immediately and permanently** from the shared folder. They
are **not** moved to a Trash or Recycle Bin and **cannot be recovered** by the app.

**You delete files at your own risk.** We are not responsible for any file that is deleted, moved,
overwritten, or lost — whether by you or by another device on your network. Keep your own backups of
anything important before sharing a folder.
